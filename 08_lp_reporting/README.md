# 08 - LP Reporting

The quarterly communication framework governing
what Meridian Ventures delivers to its LP base,
in what format, and at what standard of analytical
rigor. LP reporting is not a compliance obligation.
It is the primary mechanism through which the
blind pool trust relationship is maintained and
compounded over the fund lifecycle.

## The Reporting Standard

Meridian's LP reporting standard is calibrated
to what the most sophisticated LPs in the target
base - Asia-Pacific family office investment
committees and GCC sovereign co-investment vehicles -
require to fulfill their own governance obligations
to their principals.

A family office investment committee that has
committed to Meridian Ventures Fund I must be
able to report to the family's senior members
on the performance, risk profile, and strategic
progress of the allocation. The quarterly report
must give them everything they need to do that
without requiring them to request supplementary
information. A report that generates LP questions
about data that should have been included is a
report that has failed its primary purpose.
