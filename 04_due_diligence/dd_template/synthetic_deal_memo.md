# Due Diligence Memo - Synthetic Series A Target

**Company:** ArcWealth Pte. Ltd.
**Sector:** Wealthtech and Private Banking Infrastructure
**Jurisdiction:** Singapore
**DD Conducted:** Meridian Ventures Investment Team
**IC Submission Date:** Q1 2026
**Status:** Synthetic demonstration case

---

## Executive Summary

ArcWealth is a Singapore-incorporated wealthtech
infrastructure company providing portfolio analytics
and multi-currency reporting tools for Asia-Pacific
private banking relationship managers. The company
serves 12 private banking institutions in Singapore
and Hong Kong with a SaaS platform that consolidates
client portfolio data across custodians and generates
RM-ready client reporting in multiple languages and
currencies.

Annualized recurring revenue at IC submission date:
USD 1.8M. Gross margin: 68%. Monthly churn: 0.8%.
Cash runway: 14 months. Team: 22 staff including
three co-founders with prior experience at DBS
Private Bank and Credit Suisse Singapore.

The investment thesis: ArcWealth addresses a specific
operational pain point - fragmented portfolio data
across multiple custodians producing inconsistent
client reporting - that is acute across the Asia-Pacific
private banking market and is currently addressed
only by expensive incumbent systems designed for
Western market conventions. The product-market fit
evidence is strong. The execution team has the
relevant institutional context to sell into this
market. The competitive window is open.

**IC Recommendation:** Approve. USD 8M Series A investment
at USD 40M post-money valuation. Implied ownership: 20%.
Follow-on reserve: USD 4M for Series B participation.

## Dimension 1 - Business Model

**Overall assessment: Pass**

ArcWealth generates revenue through annual SaaS
contracts with private banking institutions. Average
contract value: USD 150K per institution per year.
12 contracted clients at IC date. Net revenue
retention of 118% reflects existing clients adding
custodian integrations and language modules to
their base contracts.

Cohort analysis across the four client cohorts
with 12 or more months of operating history shows
zero churn and average contract value expansion
of 23% at renewal. This is the most informative
signal in the DD package: existing institutional
clients are not only renewing but expanding, which
validates both product stickiness and pricing power.

Gross margin of 68% reflects a partially optimized
cost structure. At scale, the structural gross
margin is estimated at 78% to 82% as custodian
integration costs amortize across a larger client
base. This structural margin is consistent with
the 14x exit multiple assumption in the LHS model.

CAC calculated from actual sales and marketing
spend divided by new clients acquired in the prior
12 months: USD 45K per client. Against average
contract value of USD 150K, payback period is
3.6 months. This is among the strongest unit
economics observed in the Asia-Pacific B2B SaaS
market at Series A stage.

Monthly revenue churn: 0.8% on an ARR basis.
Equivalent annual churn of approximately 9.5%,
which understates retention quality because the
churned ARR reflects a single client who was
acquired through a partnership channel and had
lower product engagement than direct sales clients.

## Dimension 2 - Team Execution

**Overall assessment: Pass**

Three co-founders. CEO: 12 years at DBS Private
Bank in Singapore, most recently as Head of RM
Analytics. COO: 8 years at Credit Suisse Singapore
Private Banking Operations. CTO: 6 years at a
Singapore-based financial data infrastructure
company before ArcWealth.

The founding team has direct operational experience
in the exact pain point ArcWealth addresses. The
CEO spent 12 years building and using the fragmented
reporting tools that ArcWealth replaces. This is
the strongest possible form of founder-market fit:
the founders built ArcWealth to solve a problem
they experienced firsthand in the institutions
that are now their customers.

Key talent retention: 18 of 22 current staff have
been with the company since or within six months
of founding. The four departures were two junior
engineers who moved to hyperscalers and two
business development staff who were replaced with
more senior hires. None of the departures represented
loss of institutional knowledge.

Milestone verification: three milestones were
independently verified. First Singapore client
closed six months after founding, consistent with
the founding team's claim of a nine-month sales
cycle that was shortened to six months by the
CEO's existing institutional relationships. ARR
target of USD 1.5M for the first 18 months was
achieved at month 16. Series A fundraising initiated
at the point when runway fell below 18 months,
consistent with disciplined capital management.

Independent references: four customer references
obtained through Meridian's private banking client
network, independent of the references provided
by ArcWealth. All four confirmed that ArcWealth's
product addresses a genuine operational problem,
that the founding team is responsive to client
feedback, and that implementation timelines were
met as committed.

## Dimension 3 - Market

**Overall assessment: Pass**

Independent bottom-up market size calculation:
Asia-Pacific private banking institutions with
more than USD 5B AUM number approximately 180
across Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan, and Australia.
Average annual spend on RM analytics and client
reporting infrastructure is estimated at USD 300K
per institution based on Meridian's private banking
operational intelligence. Total addressable market
within this segment: approximately USD 54M.

Expanding to include private banking operations
at commercial banks in Indonesia, Malaysia, and
Thailand adds approximately 120 additional potential
clients at lower average contract values, bringing
total TAM to approximately USD 80M to 90M within
the five primary corridors plus secondary markets.

This TAM is smaller than the USD 1B threshold in
the target profile criteria. The IC flagged this
and conducted additional investigation. The conclusion:
ArcWealth's current product addresses the RM analytics
and reporting segment. The platform architecture
supports expansion into adjacent segments including
client onboarding workflow, regulatory reporting
automation, and client communication management.
The expanded TAM across these adjacent segments
exceeds USD 500M. Given the strong unit economics
and NRR evidence, the IC accepted this analysis
and approved IC submission with a monitoring
condition that ArcWealth's adjacent segment
expansion roadmap is reviewed at the six-month
portfolio monitoring checkpoint.

Primary competitors: three incumbent providers
were identified. All three were built for European
or North American private banking conventions
and require significant customization for Asia-Pacific
multi-currency, multi-language, and multi-custodian
requirements. ArcWealth's Asia-Pacific native
architecture is a genuine and defensible differentiation
for a minimum 24-month window, given the 12 to
18 months a well-resourced competitor would require
to rebuild the custodian integration layer for
Asia-Pacific markets.

Regulatory posture: Singapore MAS compliant.
No regulatory gaps identified. Data residency
requirements for Singapore-booked client data
are met. Hong Kong SFC requirements for the
Hong Kong client base are met.

## Dimension 4 - Financial

**Overall assessment: Pass**

Reviewed financial statements for 24-month operating
history. Revenue recognition policy consistent with
SaaS industry standards: subscription revenue
recognized ratably over contract term, setup fees
recognized over implementation period. No related
party transactions identified.

Cash position at IC date: USD 2.1M. Monthly burn:
USD 150K. Independent runway calculation: 14 months.
Consistent with founder-stated runway of 14 to 16 months.

Financial model reviewed. Growth assumptions of
80% ARR growth in Year 1 post-Series A and 60%
in Year 2 are aggressive relative to median B2B
SaaS Series A growth trajectories but are supported
by the existing pipeline of seven qualified opportunities
in active contract negotiation at IC date.

Cap table reviewed. Clean. No undisclosed instruments.
Three co-founders hold 72% combined on an as-converted
basis. Seed investors hold 18%. Employee option
pool of 10%. Post-money Series A cap table with
Meridian's 20% on a fully diluted basis is consistent
with proposed terms.

## Dimension 5 - Strategic Fit

**Overall assessment: Pass**

Co-investment suitability: high. ArcWealth's product
is directly relevant to the private banking operations
of institutions where Meridian's UHNW client base
maintains banking relationships. UHNW clients who
are sophisticated financial services investors will
recognize ArcWealth's market opportunity from direct
institutional experience. Estimated co-investment
eligible client base with relevant sector knowledge:
approximately 85 clients from the Singapore domestic
and China corridors where private banking operational
sophistication is highest.

Minimum co-investment ticket: USD 250K. Compatible
with VHNW and UHNW tier minimum investment capacity.

Private banking intelligence value: direct. A
portfolio company serving the same private banking
institutions that Meridian operates within generates
continuous ground-level intelligence about operational
technology adoption, competitor product performance,
and institutional client priorities that no market
research report captures.

Deal sourcing network value: the founding team's
prior DBS and Credit Suisse networks span Singapore
and Hong Kong private banking operations. These
networks extend Meridian's deal sourcing reach
within the wealthtech sector and within the
Singapore institutional financial services community.
