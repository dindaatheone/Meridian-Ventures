## The BI-Driven Advantage

Generalist venture funds conduct due diligence
using the same information available to every other
fund in the market: founder presentations, audited
financials, customer reference calls, and market
research reports. The information asymmetry between
funds is minimal when every fund is drawing from
the same public and semi-public information pool.

Meridian's due diligence advantage operates through
three channels unavailable to generalist funds.

The first channel is private banking client intelligence.
For every venture target in the five sector focus
areas, Meridian's private banking client base contains
institutional users, potential customers, or market
participants who can provide ground-level product
and market intelligence that no market research
report captures.

The second channel is quantitative valuation rigor.
The LHS Monte Carlo model produces a valuation
distribution rather than a point estimate. Every
assumption in that distribution is explicitly documented
in venture_assumptions.json and reviewed by the IC
before the vote. Generalist funds that rely on
comparable transaction multiples for valuation
are accepting the market's assessment of value.
Meridian builds its own.

The third channel is cross-corridor market intelligence.
Meridian's private banking operations across five
Asia-Pacific corridors generate continuous market
intelligence about regulatory developments, competitive
dynamics, and customer behavior in markets where
generalist Singapore-based funds have only periodic
exposure.
