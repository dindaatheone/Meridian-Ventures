# Investment Committee Framework - Meridian Ventures

## Composition

The Investment Committee comprises the GP principals
of Meridian Capital Management Pte. Ltd. For Fund I,
the IC operates as a three-member body:

**Managing Partner** - final authority on all investment
decisions. Chairs all IC meetings. Casts the deciding
vote in the event of a two-to-one split.

**Investment Director** - leads deal sourcing, due
diligence execution, and portfolio company monitoring.
Prepares the IC submission package for each deal.

**Risk and Valuations Officer** - owns the Monte Carlo
valuation outputs and the quantitative risk assessment
for each deal. Presents the LHS valuation range and
Expanded NPV calculation at each IC meeting.

---

## Quorum and Vote Structure

All three IC members must be present for a quorum.
Remote attendance via video is acceptable.

**New investment decisions** require unanimous approval.
A two-to-one vote does not authorize deployment.
Unanimity is required because the blind pool structure
means LPs have no deal-by-deal veto. The internal
unanimity requirement substitutes for the LP oversight
that deal-by-deal funds provide through approval rights.

**Follow-on investment decisions** (Series B participation
and bridge financing) require two-to-one majority.
Follow-on decisions benefit from existing portfolio
company performance data that reduces the decision
uncertainty relative to initial investments.

**Abandonment decisions** require unanimous agreement.
The asymmetry between new investment unanimity and
abandonment unanimity is intentional. Writing off a
position destroys LP capital and should face the
same analytical threshold as deploying it.

## IC Meeting Process

### Pre-Meeting Requirements

The Investment Director distributes the IC submission
package at least 72 hours before any scheduled vote.
The package contains: the due diligence memo from
04_due_diligence, the LHS valuation output from
Meridian-Monte-Carlo, the proposed deal terms, and
the draft decision log entry.

No IC vote proceeds without the full submission
package distributed within the required timeframe.
Emergency exceptions require Managing Partner
authorization and must be documented in the decision log.

### Meeting Structure

The meeting opens with the Risk and Valuations Officer
presenting the LHS valuation range. P10, P50, and P90
equity values are reviewed against the IC thresholds
defined in the decision log template. The Expanded
NPV is confirmed before any other discussion proceeds.

Quantitative assessment precedes qualitative discussion.
This sequencing is deliberate: presenting the valuation
range before qualitative discussion anchors the vote
on the analytical output rather than on the persuasiveness
of the presenting partner's narrative.

The Investment Director then presents the due diligence
findings across the five dimensions documented in
04_due_diligence. Each dimension receives a pass,
flag, or fail assessment. Any fail assessment requires
explicit IC discussion before the vote proceeds.

The Managing Partner facilitates the vote after all
dimensions have been presented and discussed.

### Post-Vote Requirements

The decision log entry is completed and signed within
24 hours of every IC vote, including votes that result
in rejection. Rejected deals are documented with the
same rigor as approved deals because the rejection
rationale informs future deal evaluation standards
and provides an audit trail for LP governance purposes.

---

## Conflict of Interest Policy

An IC member with a personal financial interest in
a deal under consideration, or a pre-existing relationship
with the founding team that could bias their assessment,
must disclose the conflict before the IC package is
distributed and must recuse from the vote.

A recused IC member may participate in the due diligence
discussion but cannot vote. A recusal that reduces
the voting IC to fewer than two members requires the
LP Advisory Board to appoint an independent observer
for that specific vote. The observer has no voting
rights but their presence satisfies the governance
requirement for external oversight.

---

## LP Advisory Board Interface

The LP Advisory Board is notified within five business
days of any IC decision that involves: a single
position exceeding 12% of total fund commitments,
a sector or geographic concentration approaching
the portfolio construction limits defined in the
blind pool thesis, or any conflict of interest
disclosure regardless of outcome.

The LP Advisory Board notification is informational,
not approval-seeking. Investment authority remains
with the GP. The notification fulfills the transparency
commitment made to LPs at fund close.
