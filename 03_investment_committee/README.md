## The IC as an Analytical Discipline

An investment committee exists to impose structured
analytical discipline on decisions that, without
that structure, would be vulnerable to the cognitive
biases that systematically distort individual judgment
under uncertainty: confirmation bias in evaluating
management teams, recency bias in assessing market
conditions, and anchoring bias in valuation negotiation.

The IC framework here is designed around a single
principle: every investment decision must be
analytically defensible to an LP who was not in
the room. The decision log template enforces this
by requiring that every vote produce a document
that a sophisticated LP could read and understand
the full analytical basis for the decision, including
the quantitative valuation range, the key risks
identified, and the milestone gates that will
trigger follow-on or abandonment evaluation.
