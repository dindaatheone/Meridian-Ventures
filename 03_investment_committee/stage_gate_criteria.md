# Stage Gate Criteria - Meridian Ventures Fund I

## The Gate Logic

Stage gates are not administrative checkpoints.
They are the operational mechanism through which
real options theory is implemented in practice.
Each gate is the exercise decision point for one
of the four options documented in the real options
framework: the Series A entry gate exercises the
defer option, the Series B follow-on gate exercises
the expand option, and the abandonment gate exercises
the abandon option.

The criteria at each gate are calibrated to the
specific information available at that stage and
the specific decision being made. Series A entry
criteria are designed to confirm that execution
evidence is sufficient to deploy institutional
capital. Series B follow-on criteria confirm that
the Series A execution hypothesis has been validated.
Abandonment criteria confirm that the hypothesis
has been falsified.

---

## Gate 1 - Series A Entry

**Trigger:** Initial investment decision.
**Decision:** Deploy USD 5M to 20M of Fund I capital.
**Option being exercised:** Option to defer - the
company has accumulated enough execution evidence
that waiting longer costs more in dilution and
missed upside than it saves in additional risk reduction.

### Mandatory Criteria - All Must Be Met

**Revenue threshold:** Minimum USD 500K annualized
recurring revenue at time of IC submission. Revenue
from contracted clients only. Government grants and
one-time consulting revenue excluded.

**Team assessment - pass on minimum three of four:**
At least one founder with operational experience in
Asia-Pacific financial services or technology. Evidence
of key talent retention over minimum 12-month operating
history. Absence of founding team departures that
were not disclosed proactively to investors. Quality
of internal reporting and financial discipline as
assessed by the Investment Director during DD.

**Unit economics trajectory:** Gross margin positive
or on a documented trajectory to gross margin
positivity within 12 months. Customer acquisition
cost payback period below 24 months. Monthly revenue
churn below 3% for recurring revenue contracts.

**LHS valuation:** P10 equity value must exceed the
IC minimum threshold defined in venture_assumptions.json.
Expanded NPV must be positive at the P50 case.

**Due diligence completion:** All five DD dimensions
from dd_framework.md must have been assessed. No
fail assessments may remain unresolved at the time
of the IC vote. Flag assessments must be documented
with a monitoring plan.

### Strong Preference Criteria - Inform But Do Not Determine

Singapore operational footprint or credible 12-month
relocation plan. Private banking network product fit
as defined in target_profile.md. Shariah-compliant
architecture for Indonesia or Brunei corridor companies.
Co-investment suitability for VHNW and UHNW client base.

## Gate 2 - Series B Follow-On

**Trigger:** Portfolio company approaches Series B
financing or requests bridge capital from existing investors.
**Decision:** Deploy follow-on capital from reserves
allocated at Series A, typically 50% of initial check.
**Option being exercised:** Option to expand - the
Series A execution hypothesis has been validated and
additional capital participates in a de-risked
growth trajectory.

### Mandatory Criteria - All Must Be Met

**Revenue growth:** Minimum 60% revenue growth from
Series A close to Series B evaluation date. Growth
below this threshold requires explicit IC discussion
of whether the underlying execution hypothesis
remains valid.

**Unit economics validation:** Gross margin positive
at Series B evaluation date. Customer acquisition
cost payback period improved or stable relative to
Series A. Net revenue retention above 100% for SaaS
or recurring revenue businesses, indicating that
existing customers are expanding rather than contracting.

**Valuation alignment:** Series B pre-money valuation
at or below the P75 equity value from the original
Series A LHS valuation model. If the proposed Series B
valuation exceeds the P75 case, the IC must re-run
the LHS model with updated assumptions before the
follow-on vote proceeds.

**Team continuity:** No unplanned C-suite departures
between Series A and Series B. Founding team still
operationally engaged. If a CEO transition has occurred,
the incoming CEO must have been in post for minimum
six months and must have been introduced to the IC
before the follow-on vote.

### Follow-On Sizing Logic

Follow-on capital is sized to maintain Meridian's
pro-rata ownership percentage from Series A. The
IC may elect to deploy above pro-rata in the strongest
performers where the P75 to P90 valuation case is
tracking well. Above pro-rata deployment above 1.5x
pro-rata requires unanimous IC approval and LP
Advisory Board notification.

## Gate 3 - Abandonment or Restructuring

**Trigger:** Portfolio company performance tracking
materially below plan for two consecutive quarters,
or a specific adverse event that materially changes
the investment thesis.
**Decision:** Controlled wind-down, restructured
bridge at significantly reduced valuation, or sale
of Meridian's position to a secondary buyer.
**Option being exercised:** Option to abandon - the
execution hypothesis underlying the original Series A
investment has been falsified.

### Abandonment Evaluation Triggers

Any one of the following triggers a mandatory IC
abandonment evaluation within 30 days:

Revenue decline of more than 20% in any quarter
without a documented and credible recovery plan
approved by the company's board.

Cash runway below six months without a committed
financing plan that does not require additional
Meridian capital.

Departure of two or more founding team members
within a six-month period without advance disclosure
to Meridian and a documented succession plan.

Customer churn exceeding 5% monthly for three
consecutive months.

Material regulatory adverse finding in any jurisdiction
where the company operates.

### Abandonment Decision Process

An abandonment evaluation does not automatically
result in write-off. The IC evaluates three paths:

**Path A - Bridge and restructure:** Deploy additional
capital at a materially lower valuation that reflects
the current risk profile. Appropriate when the execution
failure is recoverable and the market opportunity
remains intact.

**Path B - Secondary sale:** Sell Meridian's position
to a secondary buyer at a negotiated discount. Appropriate
when the execution failure is recoverable but Meridian's
capital is better deployed elsewhere.

**Path C - Controlled wind-down:** Work with the
founding team and other investors to maximize residual
asset recovery through IP sale, acqui-hire, or
orderly liquidation. Appropriate when the execution
failure is not recoverable.

The decision between paths requires unanimous IC
approval. The LP Advisory Board is notified of any
abandonment decision within five business days.
