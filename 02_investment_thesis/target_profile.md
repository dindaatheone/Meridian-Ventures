# Target Profile - Meridian Ventures Series A

## What Meridian Looks For

The target profile defines the characteristics that
make a Series A company investable under Meridian's
uncertainty-reduction framework. Every characteristic
is a signal of execution quality, not of idea quality.

---

## Mandatory Characteristics

### Revenue Evidence

Minimum USD 500K annualized recurring revenue at
time of investment. This threshold is not arbitrary.
It represents the minimum revenue base at which
unit economics are calculable with enough precision
to distinguish structurally sound business models
from growth funded by unsustainable acquisition spend.

Revenue quality matters as much as revenue quantity.
Recurring revenue from contracted clients carries
more signal than transactional revenue from one-time
purchases. Revenue from clients who have renewed
is more informative than revenue from clients in
their first contract period. The IC evaluates
revenue quality through customer-level cohort analysis
during due diligence.

### Team Execution Track Record

At least one founding team member must have a
demonstrable track record of operational execution
in a relevant domain within Asia-Pacific markets.
Relevant domain is defined broadly: private banking
operations, financial technology, wealth management
infrastructure, cross-border payment systems, or
Islamic finance platforms.

The track record does not require a prior successful
exit. It requires evidence of sustained operational
discipline under resource constraints: building and
retaining a competent team, managing cash runway
to planned milestones, responding to customer
feedback with product iteration, and communicating
performance transparently to existing investors.

### Market Size

Total addressable market of at least USD 1B within
the five Asia-Pacific corridors. This threshold
ensures that a portfolio company achieving 10%
market penetration at maturity creates an outcome
worth the fund's time and capital relative to
alternative deployments.

Market size estimates submitted by founders are
treated with structured skepticism during due
diligence. The DD team constructs an independent
market size estimate using a bottom-up methodology
and compares it to the founder's estimate. Material
discrepancies are investigated before IC submission.

### Unit Economics Trajectory

The company must demonstrate a clear pathway to
positive unit economics within 18 to 24 months of
the Series A close. Positive unit economics at
the time of investment is not required - many
structurally sound businesses invest ahead of
unit economics during the customer acquisition
phase. What is required is that the unit economics
trajectory is calculable, that the drivers of
improvement are within the company's operational
control, and that the timeline to positive
contribution margin is credible given the resource
plan funded by the Series A.

## Strong Preference Characteristics

### Private Banking Network Fit

Strong preference for companies whose product or
service creates direct value for, or is directly
accessible to, the Meridian private banking client
base in the five Asia-Pacific corridors. This
preference reflects the co-investment distribution
advantage and the proprietary deal sourcing
mechanisms documented in 07_pb_integration.

A wealthtech infrastructure company that Meridian's
UHNW clients will use is a better investment for
Meridian Ventures than an equally strong company
in an adjacent sector with no private banking
client connection, because the co-investment
and deal sourcing advantages are realized.

### Shariah-Compliant Architecture

For companies targeting the Indonesia and Brunei
corridors, strong preference for companies with
Shariah-compliant product architecture or a credible
roadmap to Shariah certification. This preference
reflects Meridian's client base composition:
approximately 25% of Indonesia and Brunei corridor
clients hold the Shariah-compliant flag.

### Singapore Operational Footprint

Strong preference for companies incorporated or
operating in Singapore at the time of investment.
Singapore incorporation simplifies the legal and
governance documentation required for Meridian's
fund to hold the investment, aligns the company's
regulatory environment with Meridian's own MAS
jurisdiction, and signals founder awareness of the
institutional requirements of Singapore-domiciled
institutional investors.

## Disqualifying Characteristics

The following characteristics disqualify a company
from investment consideration regardless of other strengths:

Revenue exclusively from government contracts
or grant funding. Government dependency is a
corruption of the uncertainty-reduction sequence.
It builds the appearance of revenue traction without
the market validation that makes traction informative.

Founding team with no operational experience in
Asia-Pacific markets. Business model assumptions
calibrated to US or European market dynamics
systematically underestimate the execution complexity
of Asia-Pacific customer acquisition, regulatory
navigation, and distribution channel development.

Valuation at Series A that implies a Series B
entry multiple above 20x forward revenue given
base case growth assumptions. At this valuation,
the compound option value of Series B participation
is insufficient to generate an acceptable Expanded
NPV even in the P75 scenario.
