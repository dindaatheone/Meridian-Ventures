# Sector Focus - Meridian Ventures Fund I

## Five Sectors, One Logic

Sector selection follows directly from the private
banking integration thesis. Each sector appears in
Meridian's target universe because it creates a
structural intersection between the venture deal
and the private banking client network.

A venture in a sector with no private banking client
connection is a financial investment. A venture in
a sector where Meridian's UHNW clients are both
potential users and potential co-investors is a
strategic investment. The sectors below are chosen
because they consistently produce the latter.

---

## Sector 1 - Wealthtech and Private Banking Infrastructure

**Definition:** Technology platforms that improve
the operational efficiency, client experience, or
analytical capability of private banking and wealth
management institutions.

**Private bank connection:** Meridian itself is the
target customer for products in this sector. UHNW
clients who understand private banking operations
through their own banking relationships are naturally
positioned to evaluate these products as co-investors.

**Specific opportunity areas:**
Client onboarding and KYC automation for MAS-regulated
institutions. Portfolio analytics and reporting platforms
for multi-currency Asia-Pacific portfolios. Digital
relationship management tools for private banking
RM productivity. Regulatory compliance automation
for Singapore, Hong Kong, and Indonesia financial
regulators.

**Why this sector for Meridian specifically:**
Deal sourcing advantage is maximal. Every private
banking client interaction surfaces information about
which operational pain points are most acute, which
incumbent providers are underperforming, and which
early-stage solutions are gaining traction. This
sector is where Meridian's private banking intelligence
creates the largest sourcing advantage over generalist
funds.

---

## Sector 2 - Cross-Border Payment and FX Infrastructure

**Definition:** Infrastructure enabling efficient,
compliant, and cost-effective movement of capital
across the China-Singapore, Singapore-Indonesia,
and GCC-Singapore corridors.

**Private bank connection:** Meridian's client base
transacts across five jurisdictions in five currencies.
FX conversion costs and cross-border payment friction
are real costs to these clients. UHNW clients who
have experienced this friction are credible evaluators
of solutions addressing it.

**Specific opportunity areas:**
Real-time cross-border payment rails for CNY-SGD
and IDR-SGD corridors. FX hedging platforms calibrated
to Asia-Pacific private wealth transaction profiles.
Blockchain-based settlement infrastructure for
institutional cross-border transactions.

**Why this sector for Meridian specifically:**
The client network spans exactly the corridors where
cross-border payment infrastructure is most underdeveloped
relative to transaction volume. China-Singapore, Indonesia-
Singapore, and Brunei-Singapore flows are large in
aggregate but poorly served by existing infrastructure.
Meridian's corridor presence creates both deal sourcing
and co-investment distribution advantages that generalist
Singapore venture funds cannot match.

---

## Sector 3 - Family Office Operational Platforms

**Definition:** Software and service platforms enabling
Asia-Pacific family offices to operate with institutional
discipline across investment management, reporting,
governance, and succession planning.

**Private bank connection:** UHNW clients at the
threshold of establishing or professionalizing a
family office are among the highest-value relationships
in Meridian's client base. A portfolio company that
helps these clients professionalize their family
office operations deepens the Meridian relationship
while generating venture returns.

**Specific opportunity areas:**
Multi-entity portfolio consolidation and reporting
platforms. Family governance documentation and
succession planning tools. Investment policy statement
creation and monitoring systems. Consolidated
alternative investment reporting across PE, venture,
and real estate holdings.

**Why this sector for Meridian specifically:**
The Asia-Pacific family office market is structurally
underserved by existing platforms built for North
American or European family office conventions.
Asian family office governance, succession structures,
and multi-entity reporting requirements differ
materially from Western equivalents. Platforms
built specifically for Asia-Pacific family offices
have a defensible market that Meridian's UHNW client
relationships allow it to validate with unusual
precision.

---

## Sector 4 - Islamic Finance Technology

**Definition:** Technology platforms enabling
Shariah-compliant financial products, investment
structures, and banking operations at scale.

**Private bank connection:** Approximately 25% of
Meridian's Indonesia and Brunei corridor clients
hold the Shariah-compliant flag. This client base
is underserved by conventional financial technology
platforms that do not accommodate Islamic finance
requirements. Meridian has direct access to this
client base for product validation during due
diligence and co-investment distribution.

**Specific opportunity areas:**
Shariah-compliant robo-advisory and portfolio
management platforms. Digital Sukuk issuance and
secondary market infrastructure. Islamic insurance
(Takaful) technology platforms. Zakat calculation
and distribution platforms for HNW Muslim clients.
Halal investment screening and certification tools.

**Why this sector for Meridian specifically:**
The Islamic finance technology sector is growing
rapidly across Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei, and
the GCC. Singapore's position as a regional Islamic
finance hub creates a regulatory and infrastructure
foundation for Singapore-domiciled funds to invest
in this sector. Meridian's Shariah-compliant client
base provides a proprietary validation channel that
generalist venture funds cannot replicate.

---

## Sector 5 - Real Estate Data and Analytics

**Definition:** Data platforms and analytics tools
enabling institutional and private wealth investors
to evaluate, manage, and optimize real estate
allocations across Asia-Pacific markets.

**Private bank connection:** Real estate is the
largest alternative allocation in Asia-Pacific
private wealth portfolios. UHNW clients across
the five Meridian corridors hold significant real
estate exposure in Singapore, Hong Kong, and their
home markets. Data and analytics platforms that
improve the quality of real estate investment
decisions are immediately relevant to these clients
as both potential users and co-investors.

**Specific opportunity areas:**
Prime Singapore and Hong Kong residential market
analytics platforms. Cross-border real estate
acquisition data for Chinese and Indonesian HNW buyers.
Commercial real estate performance benchmarking
tools for Asia-Pacific family office portfolios.
Real estate portfolio optimization platforms for
multi-market exposure management.

**Why this sector for Meridian specifically:**
Real estate analytics is an area where private
banking client intelligence creates maximal due
diligence advantage. Meridian's understanding of
how UHNW clients across five corridors evaluate
and acquire real estate assets - including which
data gaps are most costly to their decision-making -
allows the investment committee to assess product-market
fit with a precision unavailable to sector-agnostic funds.
