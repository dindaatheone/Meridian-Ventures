## Why This Section Defines the Institution

Any fund can document a GP/LP structure, a blind
pool thesis, and an investment committee framework.
The documentation of these elements is table stakes
for institutional credibility. What cannot be
replicated by documenting is the sourcing moat,
the co-investment distribution network, and the
AUM conversion flywheel that Meridian's private
banking client base creates.

This section documents those mechanisms in enough
operational detail that a sophisticated LP or
interviewer can understand not just that the
advantage exists but precisely how it works and
why it is structurally durable.
