# Portfolio Model - Meridian Ventures Fund I

## Fund I Synthetic Portfolio

A synthetic 12-position portfolio demonstrating
how Fund I capital is deployed across the five
corridors and five sectors within the portfolio
construction boundaries defined in the blind pool thesis.

All companies, valuations, and returns are synthetic.
This model demonstrates portfolio construction
discipline and return modeling methodology.

---

## Portfolio Construction Boundaries

| Parameter | Limit | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum single position | 15% of fund commitments | Preserves diversification across minimum 7 positions even at maximum concentration |
| Maximum corridor concentration | 40% of deployed capital | No single corridor dominates the portfolio given the five-corridor mandate |
| Stage mix | 60% Series A minimum, 40% Series B maximum | Preserves the uncertainty-reduction arbitrage window as the primary return source |
| Sector concentration | 35% maximum per sector | Prevents overexposure to a single sector's regulatory or competitive risk |
| Co-investment allocation | 20% maximum of any deal combined across LPs and clients | Preserves fund economics while creating client relationship value |

## Return Modeling

**Base case assumptions:**
30% of positions generate 5x or above gross MOIC (winners).
40% of positions generate 1.5x to 3x gross MOIC (performers).
20% of positions generate 0.5x to 1x gross MOIC (underperformers).
10% of positions return zero (write-offs).

**Base case portfolio return calculation:**

Winners (4 positions, average 6x MOIC, average USD 14.8M invested):
4 x USD 14.8M x 6x = USD 355.2M

Performers (5 positions, average 2.0x MOIC, average USD 14.8M invested):
5 x USD 14.8M x 2x = USD 148M

Underperformers (2 positions, average 0.7x MOIC, average USD 14.8M invested):
2 x USD 14.8M x 0.7x = USD 20.7M

Write-offs (1 position, average USD 14.8M invested):
1 x USD 0 = USD 0

**Total gross returns:** USD 523.9M
**Total invested:** USD 177.5M
**Gross MOIC:** 2.95x
**Gross IRR estimate:** approximately 22% to 25% over 7-year average holding period

**Net to LP after fees and carry:**
Management fees over 10-year fund life: approximately USD 28M
Gross profit above return of capital: USD 346.4M
Carry at 20% above 8% hurdle: approximately USD 65M
Net profit to LPs: approximately USD 281.4M
Net MOIC to LPs: approximately 2.4x on USD 200M committed

## Stress Scenario

**Question:** What happens to portfolio return if
three positions write off rather than one?

Write-offs (3 positions, USD 44.4M invested total):
Return: USD 0

Revised gross returns:
Winners and performers unchanged: USD 503.2M
Write-offs: USD 0
Total gross: USD 503.2M

**Revised gross MOIC:** 2.83x
**Net MOIC to LPs:** approximately 2.3x

The portfolio remains above the 2x net MOIC
level that represents a credible institutional
return for Asia-Pacific Series A-B venture exposure.
The diversification across 12 positions across
five corridors and five sectors means that write-offs
in any single sector or corridor do not produce
a fund-level failure even in an adverse scenario.
