# Blind Pool Thesis - Meridian Ventures Fund I

## The Commitment

LPs who commit to Meridian Ventures Fund I are
committing to this thesis, not to specific deals.
This document is the full investment mandate. It
defines the boundaries within which the GP will
deploy committed capital without requiring LP
approval for individual investments.

---

## Investment Mandate

**Geography:** Asia-Pacific. Five primary corridors:
China (Greater China), Singapore, Indonesia, Macau,
Brunei. Secondary corridors may be accessed where
deal quality justifies and where Meridian's private
banking client network provides sourcing advantage.

**Stage:** Series A primary target. Series B follow-on
in the strongest Series A performers. No pre-seed/seed/growth equity beyond Series B follow-on.

**Check Size:** USD 5M to USD 20M initial investment.
Follow-on reserves of up to 50% of initial check
allocated per position for Series B participation.

**Sectors:** Five focus sectors where Meridian's
private banking client network provides both deal
sourcing advantage and co-investment distribution
advantage. See 02_investment_thesis/sector_focus.md.

**Selection Principle:** Uncertainty-reduction over
idea novelty. Investments require demonstrated
execution evidence - minimum track record - before
capital is deployed. This is the application of the Bhide finding:
88% of successful ventures attribute success to
exceptional execution of ordinary ideas, not
extraordinary ideas.

---

## What Is Excluded From the Mandate

The following investment types are explicitly excluded
from the mandate regardless of return potential:

Pre-seed and seed stage companies with no revenue
evidence. The subjective uncertainty at these stages
is too high for institutional blind pool capital.

Companies outside the five primary Asia-Pacific
corridors unless the GP can document a specific
private banking client network sourcing advantage
that is unavailable to generalist funds.

Companies requiring more than USD 25M initial check
size. Position sizing discipline requires the fund
to build a diversified portfolio of 10 to 15 positions.
Concentrating more than 15% of fund capital in a
single position requires LP Advisory Board notification.

Distressed debt, real estate, and infrastructure.
These asset classes require different due diligence
and portfolio management capabilities than venture
and growth equity.

---

## Portfolio Construction Boundaries

The GP commits to maintaining these portfolio
construction boundaries throughout the deployment
period:

Maximum single position: 15% of total fund commitments.
Geographic concentration: no single corridor above 40%.
Stage mix: minimum 60% Series A, maximum 40% Series B.
Sector concentration: no single sector above 35%.
Co-investment allocation: maximum 20% of any deal
offered to LPs and eligible private banking clients combined.

---

## Reporting Commitment

LPs receive quarterly reports within 45 days of each
quarter end covering portfolio company performance,
valuation updates, pipeline activity, and risk metrics
from the Monte Carlo LP reporting model. Annual audited
financial statements within 90 days of fiscal year end.
Distribution notices within 5 business days of any
exit closing.

---

## Amendment Process

Any change to the investment mandate, portfolio
construction boundaries, or reporting commitment
requires LP Advisory Board approval by simple majority.
Changes to the fee structure or waterfall require
unanimous LP Advisory Board approval.
