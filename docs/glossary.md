# Glossary - Meridian Ventures

All terms used in this repo defined precisely and
scoped to Meridian's specific usage. Where a term
has a generic industry definition and a Meridian-
specific application, both are documented.

---

## A

**Abandonment Option**
The right but not obligation to exit a position
through controlled wind-down, secondary sale, or
bridge restructuring. In Meridian's framework, the
abandonment option is exercised when Stage Gate 3
criteria are met. The option value of being able
to abandon is included in the Expanded NPV calculation.

**AUM Conversion**
The mechanism through which venture co-investment
exit proceeds are reinvested into Meridian's private
banking wealth management mandates. Documented in
07_pb_integration/aum_conversion_model.md.

---

## B

**Bhide Finding**
The empirical finding that 88% of successful venture
founders attribute success to exceptional execution
of ordinary ideas rather than extraordinary ideas.
The primary intellectual foundation of Meridian's
uncertainty-reduction investment selection framework.

**Blind Pool**
A fund structure where LPs commit capital before
specific investments are identified. Commitment is
based on the GP's investment mandate and analytical
framework. Meridian Ventures Fund I operates as
a blind pool. The thesis documented in
01_fund_structure/blind_pool_thesis.md is the mandate
LPs commit to at fund close.

---

## C

**Carry / Carried Interest**
The GP's 20% share of fund profits above the 8%
annual hurdle rate. Paid only after LPs have received
full capital return plus the hurdle. The primary
GP incentive mechanism. Zero before the hurdle is met.

**Clawback**
The GP's obligation to return excess carry distributions
to LPs if later investments underperform such that
total fund returns fall below the hurdle. Full clawback
provisions apply to Meridian Ventures Fund I.

**Co-Investment Eligible Flag**
Boolean field in the Meridian-Business-Intelligence
client entity schema. Set to TRUE for VHNW and UHNW
tier clients. Activates co-investment offering access
for deals designated by the IC as suitable for
client co-investment participation.

**Compound Option**
An option on an option. In venture capital, Series A
capital buys the right to participate at Series B.
Series B participation buys the right to co-lead
growth rounds. Each stage is a compound option
on the next. The value of the compound option at
Series A is included in the Expanded NPV calculation.

**CVaR / Expected Shortfall**
The expected portfolio loss given that the loss
exceeds the VaR threshold. Generated by the historical
simulation model in Meridian-Monte-Carlo. A coherent
risk measure that captures tail behavior beyond
the VaR confidence level.

---

## D

**Decision Log**
The IC record produced within 24 hours of every
IC vote. Contains the full quantitative assessment,
due diligence summary, stage gate evaluation, risk
assessment, and vote outcome. Template in
03_investment_committee/decision_log_template.md.

**DPI - Distributions to Paid-In Capital**
Total cash distributions returned to LPs divided
by total capital called. The realized return metric.
DPI of 1.0x means LPs have received their capital
back. Full definition in 08_lp_reporting/performance_metrics_glossary.md.

**Dry Powder**
Committed but undeployed capital. Meridian Ventures
Fund I maintains 40% of committed capital as follow-on
reserve throughout the deployment period. Undeployed
follow-on reserve that cannot be deployed in existing
winners is returned to LPs rather than deployed
in new off-mandate positions.

---

## E

**Expanded NPV**
Static NPV plus option value. The operative valuation
metric for every IC investment decision. Includes
the compound option value of Series B participation
and the abandonment option value. Full calculation
documented in 05_valuation/valuation_framework.md.
Generated using LHS Monte Carlo from Meridian-Monte-Carlo.

---

## F

**First-Mover Commitment**
The sequential game strategy of committing irreversibly
to the boutique Asia-Pacific specialist position before
scale incumbents can profitably contest it. Documented
in 00_institutional_context/competitive_positioning.md.
The AUM concentration trigger defines the observable
market condition that signals the moment for commitment.

**Follow-On Reserve**
40% of total fund commitments reserved for Series B
participation in the strongest Series A performers.
Not available for new platform investments. Governed
by Stage Gate 2 criteria. Undepleted reserve returned
to LPs at year 6 rather than recycled into new positions.

---

## G

**GP - General Partner**
Meridian Capital Management Pte. Ltd. Makes all
investment decisions. Contributes 1% to 5% of fund
capital. Unlimited liability. Receives 20% carried
interest after LP hurdle. The decision-making entity.

**Gross IRR**
Internal rate of return on fund cash flows including
management fees but before carry. Measures investment
performance independent of fee structure. Full
definition in 08_lp_reporting/performance_metrics_glossary.md.

---

## H

**Hurdle Rate**
8% per annum compounded annually. LPs must receive
full capital return plus this preferred return before
any carried interest accrues to the GP. The minimum
return that justifies the illiquidity premium of
a 10-year locked-up fund relative to liquid alternatives.

---

## I

**IC - Investment Committee**
The three-member body with authority over all capital
deployment decisions. Composition, quorum, and vote
structure documented in 03_investment_committee/ic_framework.md.

**IRR - Internal Rate of Return**
The discount rate that makes the net present value
of all fund cash flows equal to zero. Sensitive to
cash flow timing. Must be read alongside MOIC.
Warning: leverage mechanically inflates IRR without
creating fundamental value.

---

## J

**J-Curve**
The cumulative LP cash flow pattern across a private
equity fund lifecycle. Steeply negative in years 1-3
as fees and investments are funded with no exits.
Crossing zero in years 6-7 as exits begin returning
capital. Sharply positive in years 8-10 as the
portfolio is substantially realized. Documented with
Fund I projection in 06_portfolio_construction/portfolio_model.md.

---

## L

**LHS - Latin Hypercube Sampling**
The Monte Carlo sampling method used for venture
valuation in Meridian-Monte-Carlo. Stratified sampling
ensures full coverage of input distributions with
fewer iterations than pure random sampling. The
technically correct choice for illiquid Asia-Pacific
assets with wide and asymmetric input distributions.

**LP - Limited Partner**
Institutional investors providing fund capital.
Passive. Liability limited to committed capital.
Meridian targets Asia-Pacific family offices, GCC
sovereign co-investment vehicles, and select endowments.
Full LP profile in 01_fund_structure/gp_lp_structure.md.

**LP Advisory Board**
Two to three LP representatives providing governance
oversight on conflicts of interest, fund term amendments,
and valuation disputes. No investment decision authority.

---

## M

**MOIC - Multiple on Invested Capital**
Exit proceeds or current value divided by capital
invested. Does not account for time. A 3x MOIC in
2 years is dramatically better than a 3x MOIC in
9 years. Used as a position-level return metric
alongside fund-level IRR.

---

## N

**Net IRR**
Internal rate of return from the LP's perspective
after all fees, carry, and fund expenses. The metric
compared to the 8% hurdle rate and to public market
equivalents. The return LPs actually receive.

---

## P

**PME - Public Market Equivalent**
Kaplan-Schoar PME comparing fund net cash flows to
the MSCI AC Asia Pacific Total Return Index. PME above
1.0x means the fund has outperformed the public
benchmark on a like-for-like cash flow basis. The
most important metric for assessing whether the
illiquidity premium and fee structure were justified
by genuine alpha generation.

**Proprietary Deal Flow**
Deal opportunities that surface through Meridian's
private banking client network rather than through
standard intermediary introductions or competitive
auction processes. The primary sourcing moat.
Documented in 07_pb_integration/deal_sourcing_network.md.

---

## R

**Real Option**
The right but not obligation to take a business
action in the future. In Meridian's investment process,
four real options are operationalized: the option to
defer at Series A, the compound option of follow-on
participation, the option to expand above pro-rata
in the strongest performers, and the option to abandon
when Stage Gate 3 criteria are met.

**RVPI - Residual Value to Paid-In Capital**
Reported residual NAV divided by total capital called.
The unrealized portion of TVPI. Should be declining
and converting to DPI through the realization period.

---

## S

**Sequential Game**
A competitive structure where players move in order
and can anticipate responses. Meridian's competitive
strategy against scale incumbents is a sequential
game: commit irreversibly to the boutique specialist
position, force incumbents to either contest on
boutique terms or cede the segment. Documented in
00_institutional_context/competitive_positioning.md.

**Shariah-Compliant Flag**
Boolean field in the Meridian-Business-Intelligence
client entity schema. Set to TRUE for eligible clients
in Indonesia and Brunei. Activates Islamic finance
product structuring across all mandates. Consistent
across BI, Monte Carlo, and Ventures repos.

**Stage Gate**
A defined performance threshold that must be met
before the next phase of capital deployment proceeds.
Three stage gates govern Meridian Ventures capital
deployment. Gate 1 is Series A entry. Gate 2 is
Series B follow-on. Gate 3 is the abandonment
evaluation trigger. Full criteria in
03_investment_committee/stage_gate_criteria.md.

**Style Drift**
The gradual departure from the investment mandate
that occurs when on-mandate deal flow is insufficient
to fill the deployment schedule. Prevented by the
no style drift principle in portfolio construction
and the LP Advisory Board approval requirement
for any off-mandate exception.

---

## T

**TVPI - Total Value to Paid-In Capital**
Total value (distributions plus residual NAV) divided
by total capital called. The primary fund-level return
metric. Must be read alongside IRR to account for
time value. Full definition in
08_lp_reporting/performance_metrics_glossary.md.

---

## U

**Uncertainty-Reduction Arbitrage**
The return available to an investor who deploys
capital at the precise moment when subjective
uncertainty about a venture's execution quality
has declined enough for rigorous analysis to replace
speculation, but before that execution quality is
fully priced into the market valuation. The
intellectual foundation of Meridian's Series A to B
target window. Documented in
02_investment_thesis/thesis_statement.md.

---

## V

**VaR - Value at Risk**
The maximum portfolio loss not expected to be exceeded
at a given confidence level over a given time period.
Generated by Historical Simulation Monte Carlo in
Meridian-Monte-Carlo. Presented monthly at 95% and
99% confidence in every quarterly LP report.

**Vintage Year**
The year of Meridian Ventures Fund I's first close.
Used for benchmarking against other private equity
funds with the same vintage, because market conditions
at founding affect return potential for the full fund
lifecycle.

---

## W

**Waterfall**
The distribution priority order for fund proceeds.
Step 1: full LP capital return. Step 2: 8% annual
preferred return to LPs. Step 3: GP catch-up to
20% of total profits. Step 4: 80/20 split on
remaining distributions. Full mechanics with
worked numerical example in
01_fund_structure/waterfall.md.
