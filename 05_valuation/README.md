## Why Expanded NPV

Static NPV discounts projected cash flows at a
risk-adjusted rate and produces a single point
estimate. It treats every investment as a binary
commit-or-pass decision with no intermediate
flexibility. In venture capital, that assumption
is wrong in a way that matters for capital allocation.

Venture investments are staged by design. Series A
capital does not obligate the investor to follow-on
at Series B. The decision to follow-on is made
after observing 18 to 24 months of execution evidence
that was unavailable at Series A. That optionality
has quantifiable value. A valuation framework that
ignores it systematically undervalues investments
with strong staged participation structures and
overvalues investments where the GP has no ability
to calibrate follow-on deployment to observed performance.

Expanded NPV captures that optionality. It is the
correct valuation metric for a fund whose investment
process is explicitly designed around real options
staging logic.
